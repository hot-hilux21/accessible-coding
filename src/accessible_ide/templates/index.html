The tick turns it on; the slider sets how far the other lines go down. The slider only appears once the tick is on, so the strip stays quiet for the reader who never asked for it. Bottom of the slider is off, so a reader who leaves it there is not left with lines that are faded a little while the control above them says fade is on. #} {{ (config.blur_intensity * 100) | round | int }}% {% endif %}
